Sereno Support

Sereno: White Noise & Sleep is a sleep-sound app for Android and iOS. Every soundscape is generated live on your device — there are no recordings and no streaming. The app works entirely offline and requires no account and no sign-in: install it, pick a soundscape, and rest.

Contact us

The only support channel is email, and it reaches a person, not a ticketing system:

contacto@patagoniasimracing.cl

We reply within 48 hours.

Before you write

  • Something sounds off or a feature misbehaves? Tell us your phone model and OS version, and what you expected to happen. You do not need to create an account or send us any personal data.
  • Restore a purchase? Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. Use the restore option inside the app with the same store account you bought with; if that fails, email us.
  • Your data? Everything the app stores lives only on your device, and deleting the app deletes all of it. Details in the privacy policy.
