1. What Sereno is, and what it is not

Sereno generates soundscapes — white noise, rain, wind and similar — live on your device, to help you relax and fall asleep. It also offers wind-down routines and an optional nightstand check that reads your phone's motion sensor while the phone rests beside you.

Sereno is not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any disease or condition, including any sleep disorder. It does not measure your sleep, your breathing, your heart rate or your health, and nothing it shows you is a clinical finding.

If you have persistent trouble sleeping, or you think you may have a sleep disorder such as insomnia or sleep apnea, see a qualified health professional. Do not use this App instead of medical advice, and do not use it to decide whether to start, stop or change any treatment or medication.

The nightstand check reads the motion sensor to tell whether the phone is lying still. Those readings are processed on your device and discarded. They are not a measurement of you, and they never leave the device. See the Privacy Policy.

Sereno plays audio. Protect your hearing: set the volume to a comfortable level, and be aware that sound played through headphones for a whole night at high volume can damage your hearing. Do not use the App in any situation where hearing your surroundings matters for your safety, including while driving or operating machinery.
