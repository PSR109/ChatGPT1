Privacy Policy — Sereno: White Noise & Sleep

Effective date: 2026-07-29
Applies to: Sereno: White Noise & Sleep for Android and iOS, package / bundle identifier cl.patagoniasimracing.sereno.

1. The short version

Sereno: White Noise & Sleep runs entirely on your device. Every soundscape is generated live on your phone — there are no recordings, no streaming, no account, no sign-in and no server operated by us. We do not collect, receive, store or sell your personal data, and we cannot see your mixes, your routines or your nights: they never reach us.

One thing involves someone other than you and this app: a purchase is processed by Google Play or Apple and recorded through RevenueCat (section 6). Nothing else ever leaves the device.

The rest of this document is the detail behind those statements. It deliberately does not contain clauses about data we never touch: a policy that describes collection that does not happen is not a safeguard, it is a false statement.

2. Who is responsible

The data controller is:

  • Name: Rev It Up SpA
  • Country: Chile
  • Email: contacto@patagoniasimracing.cl

The operator is a small company without a data-protection department. The email address above is the only contact channel and it reaches a person, not a ticketing system.

3. What this app does not do

To be explicit, because the absence is the point:

  • No user account, no registration, no login, no password.
  • No server, database or API of ours. The app makes no network request to any system we control, because no such system exists.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier (AAID/IDFA), no ad network SDK, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind.
  • No analytics product. The app ships with an analytics module that is a deliberate no-op: no bundled provider, no destination, so calls to it discard the event. If a future version ever sends usage data anywhere, this policy will say so by name before that version is released.
  • No microphone access, ever. Sereno generates sound; it does not record it.
  • No location access, ever.
  • No sale, rental or sharing of personal data. There is nothing to sell.
  • No email list, no newsletter, no push notifications from us.

4. What is stored, and where

Everything the app produces is written into the app's own private storage area on your device — the sandbox that Android and iOS give each installed app and that other apps cannot read.

5. The motion sensor (nightstand check)

If you use the nightstand check, Sereno reads the phone's motion sensor while it rests on your nightstand so the morning summary can describe how settled the night was. The readings are processed on the device, are never uploaded, and are not shared with anyone. The app has no access to the microphone and records no audio. You can ignore the nightstand check entirely; the rest of the app does not depend on it.

6. Purchases

Sereno is free with a starter library; Sereno Pro (monthly or yearly subscription) is sold through Google Play and the App Store. We are not the merchant of record: Google or Apple is. Your payment details are entered into Google's or Apple's systems and are never seen, handled or stored by this app or by us.

To know whether you have unlocked the paid features, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a purchase-infrastructure provider. Verified against RevenueCat's own documentation and against how this app calls their SDK:

  • What RevenueCat receives is purchase history — the transaction and receipt information needed to validate a purchase and report whether the unlock is active.
  • You are identified only by a random, anonymous identifier that RevenueCat's SDK generates on first launch. This app calls configure without supplying any user ID, so no identifier of ours, of yours, or of your device is attached to it. It is not linked to your name, your email or your Google/Apple account, and we have no way to work backwards from it to you.
  • Device identifiers and advertising attribution are opt-in features that this app does not enable. The SDK exposes collectDeviceIdentifiers() and setAttributes(). Neither is called anywhere in Sereno.
  • What we can see in RevenueCat's dashboard is aggregate commercial data — how many unlocks, in which country, at what price. Not who you are.

RevenueCat processes this data on their own infrastructure and under their own privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Google and Apple process the payment under theirs.

Separately, and outside our control: Google and Apple give developers aggregated crash and performance reports for users who have opted in, at the operating-system level, to share diagnostics with developers. Those reports come from the platform, not from code we wrote, and they contain no app content of yours.

8. Children

Sereno: White Noise & Sleep is intended for a general adult audience. It is not directed at children, is not designed for them, and contains no content, feature or advertising aimed at them. The wind-down and baby-settling guidance inside the app is written for parents and caregivers, to be used by adults, never by leaving a device with a child. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, and specifically not from a child — there is no mechanism in the app by which a child, or anyone else, could give us any.

9. Your rights and data deletion

Because we hold no personal data about you, the usual rights — access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection — have no data at our end to operate on. You exercise their practical equivalent directly: the data is already on your device, and erasure means deleting the app.

For purchase records, the holders are Google, Apple and RevenueCat. If you want the anonymous RevenueCat customer record removed, write to us at contacto@patagoniasimracing.cl and we will delete it from the RevenueCat dashboard. We cannot delete a transaction record that we do not hold and that tax and consumer law obliges the platforms to keep.
